# Brand Positioning

## Core Positioning Statement

I Craft Ads is a growth-focused digital marketing and web agency for businesses that need sharper strategy, cleaner execution and better commercial accountability across search, paid media, social, websites, Shopify and automation.

## Dubai Market Positioning

For Dubai and UAE businesses, I Craft Ads should be positioned as a practical growth partner for founders, SMEs, ecommerce brands, real-estate teams, hospitality groups and service businesses that need marketing connected to enquiries, sales and customer trust.

## One-Line Description

Digital marketing, Shopify, SEO, paid media, social and web development for brands that want marketing tied to real business goals.

## Short Elevator Pitch

I Craft Ads helps businesses turn fragmented marketing activity into a clearer growth system. The team brings together SEO, paid media, social media, Shopify, web development, email and automation so campaigns, content and websites work toward the same commercial objective.

## Long Positioning Copy

I Craft Ads works across strategy, media, content, design and technology. The agency's current service base includes SEO, Google Ads, performance marketing, social media management, Shopify development, website development, email marketing, WhatsApp and marketing automation, lead-generation campaigns and brand identity work.

The new brand direction should move away from informal agency language and toward a more direct, premium commercial position: clear diagnosis, useful execution, honest reporting and no unsupported claims.

## Brand Voice

- Direct.
- Commercially aware.
- Calmly confident.
- Clear enough for Dubai's international business market.
- Specific rather than decorative.
- Proof-led.
- Comfortable saying what still needs evidence.

## Words To Use Carefully

- Growth.
- Performance.
- Results.
- ROI.
- Leads.
- Revenue.
- Certified.
- Dubai-based.
- UAE office.

Use these only when the surrounding copy explains the method or the owner supplies evidence.

## Words And Claims To Avoid

- Number one.
- Best agency.
- Guaranteed results.
- Award-winning, unless proof is supplied.
- Top-rated, unless review proof is supplied.
- Full-service growth machine.
- Loud and proud.
- Crazy team.
- World dominators.
- Battle-tested, unless proof is supplied.

## Proof Policy

If a claim needs evidence, write the content with one of these labels:

- [OWNER MUST CONFIRM]
- [OWNER MUST PROVIDE VERIFIED RESULT]
- [OWNER MUST PROVIDE CLIENT PERMISSION]
- [OWNER MUST PROVIDE ASSET]
- [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]
