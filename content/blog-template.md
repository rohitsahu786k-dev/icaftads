# Blog Template

## Publication Rule

Blog content should be useful, current and specific. Do not invent search volume, costs, market statistics or performance results.

## Front Matter Template

```yaml
title: "[Article title]"
slug: "[article-slug]"
primary_keyword: "[Primary keyword]"
secondary_keywords:
  - "[Secondary keyword]"
  - "[Secondary keyword]"
intent: "[Informational / commercial investigation / comparison]"
related_service: "[Service URL]"
status: "draft"
last_reviewed: "[DATE]"
```

## Article Structure

### H1

Use the article title.

### Introduction

State who the article is for and what decision it helps them make.

### Quick Answer

Give the direct answer early.

### Main Sections

Use clear sections based on the search intent:

1. What the topic means.
2. Why it matters in Dubai/UAE.
3. What affects cost, performance or scope.
4. What to check before hiring an agency.
5. What I Craft Ads can help with.
6. Next step.

### Evidence And Sources

Use sources where the article discusses current rules, platform requirements, legal/compliance topics or market facts.

### Internal Links

Include:

- One link to the relevant service page.
- One link to a related insight.
- One link to contact where useful.

### CTA

If you are making this decision for your business, tell us what you are trying to improve and we will help you choose the right next step.
