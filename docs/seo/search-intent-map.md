# Search Intent Map

## Research Limitations

Current search sampling identified ranking page types and competitor patterns. It did not provide reliable search volume, ranking difficulty, paid ad counts, traffic estimates or a visual local-pack view. Those must be gathered later through Google Search Console, Google Ads Keyword Planner, Ahrefs/Semrush, manual Google.ae checks and Google Business Profile data where available.

## Query Intent Analysis

### Digital Marketing Agency Dubai

- Intent: Buyer wants a shortlist of agencies.
- Funnel stage: Bottom/mid funnel.
- Ranking page type: Homepage or broad service landing page.
- Required page: Homepage.
- Content required:
  - Clear Dubai/UAE relevance.
  - Core service pillars.
  - Proof, testimonials and case studies if approved.
  - Contact path.
  - Honest explanation of markets served.
- Avoid:
  - Fake "Dubai office" claim.
  - Generic "best agency" headline.

### Digital Marketing Company Dubai

- Intent: Same as agency query, often broader vendor comparison.
- Funnel stage: Bottom/mid funnel.
- Ranking page type: Homepage, agency landing pages, directories.
- Required page: Homepage, not a duplicate page.
- Content required:
  - Company credibility.
  - Services.
  - Industries.
  - Contact.

### Performance Marketing Agency Dubai

- Intent: Buyer wants accountable paid/acquisition growth.
- Funnel stage: Bottom funnel.
- Ranking page type: Performance service page.
- Required page: Dedicated service page if active.
- Content required:
  - Channels: Google, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok where real.
  - Tracking and reporting.
  - Landing-page/CRO link.
  - Budget and testing approach.
  - Case studies if available.

### SEO Agency Dubai

- Intent: Buyer wants search visibility, rankings, traffic or leads.
- Funnel stage: Bottom/mid funnel.
- Ranking page type: SEO service page.
- Required page: Dedicated SEO page.
- Content required:
  - Technical SEO, on-page SEO, local SEO, content strategy.
  - Audit process.
  - Reporting cadence.
  - FAQs around timeline and cost.
  - Evidence before any ranking/traffic claims.

### Google Ads Agency Dubai

- Intent: Buyer needs campaign setup/management or a better PPC partner.
- Funnel stage: Bottom funnel.
- Ranking page type: Google Ads/PPC service page.
- Required page: Paid media page or Google Ads page.
- Content required:
  - Account audit.
  - Search, Performance Max, display, shopping, YouTube where relevant.
  - Conversion tracking.
  - Budget control and optimization.
  - Platform certifications if verified.

### PPC Agency Dubai

- Intent: Paid media vendor comparison.
- Funnel stage: Bottom funnel.
- Ranking page type: PPC service pages and directories.
- Required page: Paid media page.
- Content required:
  - Google/Bing/Meta/LinkedIn campaign coverage.
  - Landing-page quality.
  - Tracking and reporting.
  - Lead quality and sales handoff.

### Social Media Agency Dubai

- Intent: Buyer wants social content, social ads, influencer or community management.
- Funnel stage: Mid/bottom funnel.
- Ranking page type: Social media service page.
- Required page: Dedicated social media page if active.
- Content required:
  - Strategy.
  - Content production.
  - Community management.
  - Paid social.
  - Influencer/creator support if actually offered.
  - Creative examples.

### Web Design Company Dubai

- Intent: Buyer needs a new website, redesign or development partner.
- Funnel stage: Bottom funnel.
- Ranking page type: Web design/development page, portfolio pages, directories.
- Required page: Dedicated web design/development page.
- Content required:
  - Discovery and UX.
  - SEO-ready architecture.
  - Performance and accessibility.
  - CMS/Next.js/WordPress/Shopify capability as real.
  - Portfolio.
  - Timeline and quote process.

### Shopify Development Dubai

- Intent: Buyer needs Shopify setup, redesign, migration or Shopify Plus support.
- Funnel stage: Bottom funnel.
- Ranking page type: Shopify specialist landing pages, ecommerce agency pages, directories.
- Required page: Dedicated Shopify/ecommerce page if service is active.
- Content required:
  - Store setup.
  - Theme/custom development.
  - Payment/logistics/app setup.
  - SEO and analytics.
  - CRO and retention.
  - UAE ecommerce specifics.

### Lead Generation Company Dubai

- Intent: Buyer wants enquiries, calls, sales leads or appointments.
- Funnel stage: Bottom funnel.
- Ranking page type: Lead-gen specialist pages.
- Required page: Dedicated lead-generation page if offer is concrete.
- Content required:
  - Channels used.
  - Lead qualification.
  - CRM/form/WhatsApp handoff.
  - Compliance.
  - Reporting.
  - Case-study proof.

### Branding Agency Dubai

- Intent: Buyer needs brand strategy, naming, identity or rebrand.
- Funnel stage: Mid/bottom funnel.
- Ranking page type: Brand agency pages and portfolio pages.
- Required page: Branding page only if real portfolio exists.
- Content required:
  - Strategy deliverables.
  - Identity deliverables.
  - Visual examples.
  - Brand guidelines.
  - Case studies.

### Marketing Automation Agency Dubai

- Intent: Buyer wants CRM, HubSpot, workflows, email/SMS/WhatsApp automation or lead nurturing.
- Funnel stage: Mid/bottom funnel.
- Ranking page type: HubSpot/automation partner pages and automation service pages.
- Required page: Dedicated automation page only if tools/process are real.
- Content required:
  - Platform expertise.
  - Workflow examples.
  - Lead nurturing.
  - CRM integrations.
  - Reporting and consent/compliance.

## Local Pack Presence

The available search tool did not expose a visual Google map pack. Because many organic competitors publish UAE phone numbers, addresses, Google reviews and local agency listings, manual verification should be done before final SEO planning:

- Search each core query in Google.ae from Dubai.
- Capture whether a map pack appears.
- Record top 3 map-pack businesses, ratings, review counts, categories and addresses.
- Confirm whether I Craft Ads is eligible for a UAE Google Business Profile.

Do not add LocalBusiness schema or a UAE address until actual UAE business facts are confirmed.

## Page Creation Rule

Do not create several near-identical Dubai location pages. Use one strong page per distinct intent:

- One homepage for broad agency/company intent.
- One page per core service only when the service has unique depth.
- One industry page only when I Craft Ads has real industry experience or a distinct playbook.
- Case-study pages only for approved, real client work.
- Blog pages for informational questions that do not duplicate service pages.
