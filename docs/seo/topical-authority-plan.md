# Topical Authority Plan

## Strategic Position

I Craft Ads should build topical authority around practical growth marketing for Dubai and UAE businesses, not generic global agency language.

Recommended authority pillars:

1. Dubai digital growth strategy.
2. SEO and search visibility.
3. Paid media and performance marketing.
4. Web design, conversion and development.
5. Shopify and ecommerce growth.
6. Lead generation and marketing automation.
7. Brand and social presence.

## Launch Page Set

### Homepage

Target:

- digital marketing agency Dubai.
- digital marketing company Dubai.

Purpose:

- Capture broad commercial intent.
- Route buyers to service pages, industries, proof and contact.

Evidence needed:

- Real UAE operating status.
- Contact details.
- Approved testimonials/client proof.

### Core Service Pages

1. `/services/seo/`
   - Targets: SEO agency Dubai, SEO company Dubai, SEO services Dubai.
   - Must include: audit, technical SEO, content, local SEO, reporting, realistic timeline.

2. `/services/paid-media/`
   - Targets: PPC agency Dubai, Google Ads agency Dubai, performance marketing agency Dubai.
   - Must include: Google Ads, Meta/paid social if active, tracking, testing, reporting.

3. `/services/social-media-marketing/`
   - Targets: social media agency Dubai.
   - Must include: strategy, content systems, community, paid social, reporting.

4. `/services/web-design-development/`
   - Targets: web design company Dubai, website development company Dubai.
   - Must include: UX, SEO-ready builds, performance, accessibility, platform choices.

5. `/services/shopify-development/`
   - Targets: Shopify development Dubai, Shopify agency Dubai.
   - Must include: setup, redesign, migration, apps, payments, analytics, CRO.

6. `/services/lead-generation-automation/`
   - Targets: lead generation company Dubai, marketing automation agency Dubai.
   - Must include: channels, CRM handoff, workflows, email/WhatsApp/SMS where real, compliance.

7. `/services/branding/`
   - Targets: branding agency Dubai.
   - Launch only if owner supplies brand work samples and permissions.

## Industry Pages

Create industry pages only if I Craft Ads can supply real examples, process knowledge or proof. Do not make generic location pages.

Recommended candidates:

- `/industries/ecommerce-marketing-dubai/`
  - Strongest fit with Shopify/D2C content.
- `/industries/real-estate-marketing-dubai/`
  - Create only with real estate experience and lead-quality process.
- `/industries/hospitality-marketing-dubai/`
  - Create only with approved hospitality examples.
- `/industries/professional-services-marketing-dubai/`
  - Create only if service-firm client experience exists.

## Case Study Pages

Case studies should be the primary proof layer once approved.

Recommended format:

- Client context.
- Market and challenge.
- Scope.
- Channels.
- Timeline.
- Constraints.
- Work delivered.
- Outcomes with verified metrics only.
- Client quote with permission.

Case-study targets:

- Dubai SEO case study.
- Dubai Google Ads case study.
- Dubai Shopify development case study.
- Dubai lead generation case study.
- Dubai web design case study.

## Content Gap Opportunities

- Honest proof-first agency selection content.
- Dubai SME/founder-specific guidance rather than enterprise-only positioning.
- UAE ecommerce operations content: payments, delivery, Arabic/English UX and retention.
- Measurement and CRM handoff content for lead quality.
- Non-hype AI/automation content focused on workflows and governance.

## Evidence Rules

Do not publish:

- "Best" or "#1" claims unless independently verified and legally safe.
- Google/Meta/HubSpot/Shopify partner claims without proof.
- Client counts, revenue influenced, lead volumes, ROAS, traffic growth or rankings without evidence.
- LocalBusiness schema or address without real UAE facts.
- Client logos or testimonials without permission.
