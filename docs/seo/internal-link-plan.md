# Internal Link Plan

## Principles

- Link by user intent, not by keyword repetition.
- Avoid multiple near-identical Dubai pages.
- Use descriptive, natural anchor text.
- Make proof pages central once approved.
- Keep service pages connected to relevant industries, FAQs and case studies.

## Proposed Hub Structure

### Homepage

Links to:

- SEO.
- Paid Media.
- Social Media Marketing.
- Web Design & Development.
- Shopify Development.
- Lead Generation & Automation.
- Branding, only if approved.
- Work/Case Studies.
- Insights.
- Contact.

Recommended anchors:

- SEO for Dubai growth.
- Paid media and Google Ads.
- Shopify and ecommerce development.
- Lead generation systems.
- Website design and development.

### Services Hub

Purpose:

- Summarize all services.
- Help buyers choose the right path.
- Link to every service page.

Cross-links:

- Paid Media links to Web Design for landing pages.
- Paid Media links to Lead Generation for CRM and qualification.
- SEO links to Web Design for technical fixes.
- Shopify links to SEO, Paid Media and Automation.
- Social links to Branding and Paid Media.
- Branding links to Web Design and Social.

### Work / Case Studies Hub

Purpose:

- Central proof repository.
- Link from every service page once cases exist.

Case-study links:

- SEO page to SEO case studies.
- Paid Media page to Google Ads/PPC case studies.
- Shopify page to ecommerce case studies.
- Web Design page to website redesign cases.
- Lead Generation page to lead-quality cases.

Do not create case-study pages without real approved client details.

### Industry Pages

Only create when evidence exists.

Recommended links:

- Ecommerce industry page links to Shopify, SEO, Paid Media, Email/Automation and ecommerce cases.
- Real estate industry page links to Lead Generation, PPC, SEO and real estate cases.
- Hospitality industry page links to Social, Web Design, SEO and hospitality cases.
- Professional services page links to SEO, Google Ads, Web Design and lead-gen cases.

### Blog / Insights

Use informational posts to support service pages:

- "How to choose a digital marketing agency in Dubai" links to homepage, services hub and contact.
- "SEO cost in Dubai" links to SEO service page.
- "Google Ads agency vs freelancer" links to Paid Media.
- "Shopify development cost Dubai" links to Shopify.
- "Landing page checklist" links to Web Design and Paid Media.
- "Lead tracking checklist" links to Lead Generation & Automation.

## Footer Links

Recommended footer groups:

- Services.
- Industries, only approved pages.
- Work.
- Insights.
- Company.
- Legal.
- Contact.

Do not include UAE address or phone until confirmed.

## Breadcrumbs

Use breadcrumbs for:

- Service pages.
- Industry pages.
- Case studies.
- Blog posts.

Example:

- Home > Services > SEO.
- Home > Work > Client SEO Case Study.

## Anchor Text Guidance

Use:

- "SEO strategy for Dubai businesses."
- "Google Ads and paid media."
- "Shopify development for UAE ecommerce."
- "Lead generation and automation."
- "Website design and development."

Avoid:

- Repeating exact-match "best digital marketing agency Dubai" across the site.
- Footer stuffing every Dubai keyword.
- Linking to duplicate location pages.
