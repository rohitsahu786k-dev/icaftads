# 90-Day Dubai SEO Growth System

## Principles

- No traffic forecasts, ranking guarantees or invented benchmark claims.
- Prioritise service pages, proof, technical quality and useful Dubai/UAE buyer education.
- Publish only content that includes expert input, current business facts and a clear conversion path.
- Use Google Business Profile only if I Craft Ads is eligible with a real, compliant business presence.

## Operating Cadence

| Cadence | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly | Search Console checks, query/page analysis, form and conversion review, index coverage triage. |
| Biweekly | Internal-link updates, service-page improvements, content brief reviews, directory consistency checks. |
| Monthly | Technical crawl, Core Web Vitals review, redirect/404 review, competitor SERP notes, content performance review. |
| Quarterly | Case-study pipeline review, digital PR targets, service-page repositioning, conversion-rate roadmap. |

## Days 1-30: Measurement, Indexing and Commercial Foundations

### Search Console and Index Coverage

- Verify the final domain property in Search Console.
- Submit `/sitemap.xml` after production launch approval.
- Check Pages report for indexed, crawled-not-indexed, discovered-not-indexed, redirect and noindex issues.
- Confirm canonical URLs use the final production domain.
- Check `robots.txt` and noindex behavior for `/admin`, `/preview`, `/search` and parameter URLs.
- Create a weekly log of pages with impressions, clicks, CTR and average position. Do not treat early data as a forecast.

### Query and Landing-Page Analysis

- Export queries by landing page weekly once impressions appear.
- Group queries by intent: agency selection, service comparison, cost research, implementation guidance, brand/navigation.
- Identify pages getting mismatched queries, then adjust headings, FAQs, internal links or CTAs.
- Watch for cannibalisation between homepage, services hub and service pages.

### Technical Crawl

- Run a full crawl at launch and again at day 30.
- Check status codes, canonicals, title/meta duplication, H1 count, noindex, structured data, image alt text, internal links and orphan pages.
- Compare crawl results with Search Console coverage.

### Core Web Vitals

- Monitor CrUX/Search Console Core Web Vitals when enough field data exists.
- Use Lighthouse only as lab support; prioritise real-user 75th percentile data once available.
- Track LCP, INP and CLS by template: homepage, service page, article, contact.

### Service-Page Enhancements

Priority pages:

- `/services/seo-dubai`
- `/services/ppc-google-ads-dubai`
- `/services/shopify-development-dubai`
- `/services/web-design-development-dubai`
- `/services/lead-generation-marketing-automation-dubai`

Enhance each with:

- Specific Dubai/UAE use cases.
- Process detail.
- Proof slots for approved screenshots or anonymised examples.
- FAQs based on buyer objections.
- Stronger internal links to related articles and case studies.
- One primary conversion CTA and one proof/education secondary CTA.

## Days 31-60: Useful Content, Internal Links and Proof

### Content Production

Publish only articles with owner or practitioner input. Each article should include examples, decision frameworks, checklists or local operating details that a generic AI article would not know.

Suggested sequence:

1. Digital marketing costs in Dubai.
2. SEO vs Google Ads for Dubai businesses.
3. Choosing a Shopify agency in Dubai.
4. UAE landing-page conversion optimisation.

### Internal-Link Improvements

- Add contextual links from articles to the most relevant service pages.
- Add “related reading” links between articles in the same buyer journey.
- Add links from service pages to articles that answer objections.
- Add links from case studies back to the service page, related article and contact form.
- Review anchor text for clarity; avoid repetitive exact-match spam.

### Case-Study Publishing

Prepare at least one approved case study if the owner can provide:

- Client permission or approved anonymisation.
- Client context, market, challenge and scope.
- Work delivered and dates.
- Screenshots with sensitive data removed.
- Verified outcomes only if baseline, timeframe and measurement method are documented.
- Approved testimonial quote if used.

If proof is not ready, publish no case study. Improve `/work` with “proof standards” instead.

### Business Directory Consistency

- Audit existing listings for name, website, service categories, phone and address consistency.
- Fix only verified business details.
- Prioritise reputable UAE/business directories and platforms where buyers actually validate agencies.
- Avoid bulk low-quality directory submissions.

### Google Business Profile

Use GBP only if eligible:

- Real business name.
- Real service-area or storefront eligibility.
- Accurate phone, website, category and address/service-area details.
- Ability to receive verification and manage reviews.

Do not add LocalBusiness schema, map-pack claims or a UAE address until eligibility and business facts are confirmed.

## Days 61-90: Authority, CRO and Compounding

### Digital PR and Link Acquisition

Focus on credible reasons to earn mentions:

- Owner commentary on UAE ecommerce, paid media measurement, SEO proof standards or lead-quality systems.
- Practical data-free guides with expert perspective.
- Guest contributions to relevant UAE business, ecommerce, startup or marketing publications.
- Partner/vendor profile pages only where the partnership is real.
- Case-study co-promotion with client approval.

Avoid paid link schemes, irrelevant guest posts, fake awards and directory spam.

### Conversion-Rate Optimisation

- Review Search Console landing pages with impressions but low conversion engagement.
- Review analytics events: `strategy_call_click`, `contact_form_start`, `contact_form_submit`, `form_error`.
- Improve CTA clarity by page intent.
- Test contact form friction: required fields, service options, budget/timeline labels and error messaging.
- Add proof or reassurance near CTAs: process, privacy, response expectation, relevant case study.
- Monitor mobile form completion separately.

### Monthly Technical Crawl

At day 60 and day 90:

- Crawl all indexable pages.
- Check legacy 301s and 404s.
- Confirm sitemap includes only canonical indexable URLs.
- Confirm robots rules still match launch policy.
- Check broken internal/external links.
- Check Core Web Vitals field data when available.
- Review structured data and Search Console enhancements.

### Day-90 Review

Produce a plain-language review:

- Pages indexed and excluded.
- Queries emerging by landing page.
- Content published and internally linked.
- Case studies approved/pending.
- Conversion events and form issues.
- Technical issues fixed/open.
- Next 90-day priorities.

## Measurement Without Forecasts

Track directional evidence only:

- Pages indexed.
- Queries appearing.
- Impressions by page/query group.
- CTR changes after title/meta updates.
- Service-page engagement and CTA clicks.
- Form starts, errors and submissions.
- Internal links added and crawl depth improved.
- Case studies approved and published.
- Referring domains and quality mentions earned.

Do not publish or promise ranking positions, lead volumes, traffic growth percentages, ROAS, revenue
impact or timelines unless verified after the fact with approved data and clear methodology.
