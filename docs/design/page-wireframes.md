# Page Wireframes

## Purpose

These are responsive high-fidelity structural wireframes for Phase 5. They define layout behavior before visual implementation.

## Global Layout Rules

Desktop:

- 12-column grid.
- 1440px max container.
- 48px to 64px page margins.
- Large editorial section spacing.

Tablet:

- 8-column grid.
- 32px page margins.
- Reduce headline scale but keep hierarchy strong.

Mobile:

- 4-column grid.
- 20px page margins.
- Intentional stacking.
- Sticky or repeated CTA where useful.
- Avoid tiny cards and cramped multi-column layouts.

## Homepage

### Desktop

1. Header.
   - Left brand.
   - Center/right nav.
   - Right CTA.
2. Hero.
   - Left 7 columns: kicker, oversized H1, lead, CTA row.
   - Right 5 columns: approved media or static abstract visual.
   - Bottom hint of credibility row visible.
3. Credibility.
   - Fine line top.
   - Approved logos or proof placeholders clearly labelled.
4. Business outcomes.
   - 4-column outcome strip.
   - No fake numbers.
5. Services.
   - Large section heading.
   - 2x3 service grid.
   - Fine borders.
6. Selected work.
   - Feature case on left.
   - Two smaller cases on right.
   - Use placeholders until approved.
7. Results.
   - Only with verified numbers.
   - Otherwise use "Proof pending owner approval" internal state.
8. Dubai market understanding.
   - Text-heavy editorial band.
   - Optional city/market image only if real or appropriately licensed.
9. Working process.
   - Horizontal 4-step line.
10. Testimonials.
    - 3 cards if permission exists.
11. Insights.
    - 3 article cards.
12. Final CTA.
    - Full-width black-2 band.
    - Large heading and contact CTA.
13. Footer.

### Tablet

- Hero becomes 5/3 columns or stacked depending viewport.
- Services grid becomes 2 columns.
- Selected work becomes one featured card plus two stacked cards.
- Process becomes 2x2.
- Testimonials become horizontal scroll only if accessible; otherwise stacked.

### Mobile

- Header becomes brand + menu + CTA icon/text.
- Hero stacks: text first, media second.
- H1 remains large but not clipped.
- Outcome cards stack as full-width rows.
- Services become single-column cards with stable heights.
- Selected work stacks with clear labels.
- Process becomes vertical timeline.
- Final CTA appears before footer with one button.

## Service Landing Page

Applies to SEO, PPC/Google Ads, Social, Web, Shopify and Lead Generation/Automation.

### Desktop

1. Header.
2. Service hero.
   - Kicker.
   - H1.
   - Lead copy.
   - CTA.
   - Side panel: service summary, best-fit audience, proof requirements.
3. Problem section.
   - Large editorial statement.
   - 3 to 4 common buyer problems.
4. What we do.
   - Structured service modules.
   - Each module has action and business relevance.
5. Process.
   - Diagnose, Plan, Build, Improve.
6. Proof.
   - Case-study links or explicit proof-needed state.
7. FAQs.
   - 5 to 7 page-specific questions.
8. Related services.
   - 2 to 3 cross-links.
9. Final CTA.
10. Footer.

### Tablet

- Hero side panel moves below lead copy.
- What-we-do modules become 2 columns.
- Related services become 2 columns or stacked.

### Mobile

- Service summary panel appears immediately after CTA.
- What-we-do modules stack.
- FAQs use accessible accordion.
- Sticky bottom CTA may be used if it does not cover content.

## Insights Article

### Desktop

1. Header.
2. Article hero.
   - Category.
   - H1.
   - Excerpt.
   - Reviewed date.
3. Body shell.
   - Left 3 columns: table of contents.
   - Center 7 columns: article body.
   - Right 2 columns: related CTA or service link.
4. Inline callouts.
5. FAQ section if useful.
6. Related articles.
7. Service CTA.
8. Footer.

### Tablet

- TOC moves above article body.
- Right CTA becomes inline block after intro.

### Mobile

- Article body single column.
- TOC is collapsible or inline.
- Keep paragraphs narrow and readable.
- CTAs appear after meaningful sections, not after every paragraph.

## Contact Page

### Desktop

1. Header.
2. Contact hero.
   - H1.
   - Short qualification copy.
3. Two-column contact area.
   - Left: form.
   - Right: contact details, service fit, response expectations.
4. Privacy note.
5. Related service links.
6. Footer.

### Tablet

- Form remains first.
- Contact details move below or into a right 3-column panel if space allows.

### Mobile

- Form is first after intro.
- Fields are full width.
- Service select uses native control.
- Consent text remains readable.
- Contact details are clearly labelled and only show confirmed information.

## Mobile Quality Rules

- No text overlaps.
- No horizontal scrolling.
- Buttons are at least 44px tall.
- Section headings fit within viewport.
- Cards do not nest inside other cards.
- Media has fixed aspect ratios to avoid layout shift.
- CTA remains visible but does not cover form fields or footer links.
