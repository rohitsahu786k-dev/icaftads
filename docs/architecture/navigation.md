# Navigation

## Navigation Goals

- Help buyers identify the right service quickly.
- Keep broad Dubai agency intent on the homepage.
- Avoid duplicate location pages.
- Prioritize conversion and proof.
- Keep conditional pages out of primary navigation until evidence exists.

## Recommended Header Navigation

Primary items:

1. Services.
2. Work.
3. Insights.
4. About.
5. Contact.

Primary CTA:

- Book a strategy call.

## Services Menu

Recommended dropdown or services landing links:

- SEO.
- PPC & Google Ads.
- Social Media Marketing.
- Web Design & Development.
- Shopify Development.
- Lead Generation & Automation.

Conditional:

- Branding.
- Marketing Automation as standalone.
- Performance Marketing as standalone.

Do not include conditional pages in the header until proof and unique content exist.

## Footer Navigation

### Services

- SEO Dubai.
- PPC & Google Ads Dubai.
- Social Media Marketing Dubai.
- Web Design & Development Dubai.
- Shopify Development Dubai.
- Lead Generation & Automation Dubai.
- Branding, conditional.

### Company

- About.

- Insights.
- Contact.

### Legal

- Privacy Policy.
- Terms.

### Contact

- Use only confirmed phone, email, address and social links.
- If Dubai/UAE office status is unconfirmed, do not publish a UAE office label.

## Mobile Navigation

Mobile should prioritize:

1. Services.
2. Work.
3. Contact.
4. Insights.
5. About.

CTA should remain easy to reach:

- Book a strategy call.

## Breadcrumb Strategy

Use breadcrumbs on:

- Service pages.
- Case studies.
- Insight articles.
- Legal pages.

Examples:

- Home > Services > SEO Dubai.
- Home > Work > Shopify Development Case Study.
- Home > Insights > SEO Cost in Dubai.

## Cross-Navigation Rules

- Homepage links to every core service.
- Service pages link laterally only when there is a real relationship.
- Shopify links to SEO, PPC and automation.
- PPC links to web design and lead-generation/automation.
- SEO links to web design and Shopify.
- Social links to branding only if branding launches.
- Work links back to relevant service pages.
- Insights link to service pages sparingly and contextually.

## Avoid

- Footer keyword stuffing.
- Repeating "Dubai" in every anchor unnaturally.
- Multiple similar location pages.
- "Best agency" navigation labels.
- Client/portfolio links if there is no approved proof yet.
