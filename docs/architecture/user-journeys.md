# User Journeys

## Journey Principles

- Every journey should move from clear diagnosis to proof to a low-friction inquiry.
- Do not rely on fake stats, fake client logos or fake Dubai contact details.
- Use service pages to clarify fit before asking for a consultation.
- Use case studies only when approved proof exists.

## Journey 1: Dubai Business Owner

### User Context

A founder or owner of a Dubai SME wants more reliable growth but may not know whether they need SEO, ads, social, a new website or lead generation.

### Primary Path

1. Lands on `/` from "digital marketing agency Dubai."
2. Scans positioning, services and proof.
3. Clicks `/services`.
4. Chooses a relevant service page or compares pillars.
5. Reviews `/work` if proof is available.
6. Clicks `/contact`.
7. Submits inquiry.

### Supporting Path

1. Lands on `/insights/choose-digital-marketing-agency-dubai`.
2. Reads checklist.
3. Clicks `/services`.
4. Clicks `/contact`.

### Conversion Triggers

- "Book a strategy call."
- "Find the right service."
- "Tell us what growth needs to mean next."

### Required Trust Signals

- Confirmed UAE operating status.
- Clear services.
- Approved testimonials.
- Real case studies if available.
- Transparent process.

### Friction To Remove

- Confusing service overlap.
- Fake or vague ROI claims.
- Missing phone/form destination.
- Unclear Dubai/UAE status.

## Journey 2: E-commerce Founder

### User Context

An ecommerce founder or D2C brand needs a Shopify store build, redesign, CRO, paid media or retention support.

### Primary Path

1. Lands on `/services/shopify-development-dubai` from "Shopify development Dubai."
2. Reviews Shopify services, UAE ecommerce considerations and examples.
3. Clicks related `/services/ppc-google-ads-dubai` or `/services/seo-dubai`.
4. Reviews Shopify/ecommerce case study under `/work/[case-study-slug]`.
5. Clicks `/contact`.
6. Submits store brief.

### Supporting Path

1. Lands on `/insights/shopify-development-cost-dubai`.
2. Reads cost/planning guide.
3. Clicks Shopify service page.
4. Clicks contact.

### Conversion Triggers

- "Discuss my Shopify store."
- "Start a website brief."
- "Build a similar plan."

### Required Trust Signals

- Approved Shopify screenshots.
- Real store examples.
- Shopify Partner proof only if true.
- Payment/logistics/app setup expertise.
- Case metrics only if verified.

### Friction To Remove

- Generic web design copy.
- No store examples.
- Unsupported revenue claims.
- No clarity on platform scope.

## Journey 3: Lead-Generation Client

### User Context

A B2B, real estate, professional services or local business wants more qualified enquiries, not just traffic.

### Primary Path

1. Lands on `/services/lead-generation-marketing-automation-dubai` from "lead generation company Dubai."
2. Reviews funnel, channels, qualification and CRM handoff.
3. Clicks `/services/ppc-google-ads-dubai` for acquisition channel detail.
4. Clicks `/work/[case-study-slug]` if approved lead-gen case exists.
5. Clicks `/contact`.
6. Submits lead-generation brief.

### Supporting Path

1. Lands on `/services/ppc-google-ads-dubai`.
2. Clicks lead-generation/automation cross-link.
3. Clicks contact.

### Conversion Triggers

- "Map my lead funnel."
- "Audit my ad account."
- "Send inquiry."

### Required Trust Signals

- Lead-quality methodology.
- Tracking/reporting process.
- CRM or form handling proof.
- Compliance position for WhatsApp/email/SMS.
- Verified lead outcomes before numbers.

### Friction To Remove

- Promising lead volume without proof.
- No definition of a qualified lead.
- No CRM/form destination.
- No privacy/consent language.

## Journey 4: Website Development Prospect

### User Context

A business needs a new website, redesign, landing page or development partner. They care about credibility, speed, SEO and conversion.

### Primary Path

1. Lands on `/services/web-design-development-dubai` from "web design company Dubai."
2. Reviews process, platform options, SEO/performance/accessibility commitments.
3. Clicks `/work` for examples.
4. Clicks related `/services/seo-dubai` or `/services/ppc-google-ads-dubai`.
5. Clicks `/contact`.
6. Submits website brief.

### Supporting Path

1. Lands on an approved website case study.
2. Clicks web design service page.
3. Clicks contact.

### Conversion Triggers

- "Start a website brief."
- "See what we can improve for you."

### Required Trust Signals

- Portfolio screenshots.
- Process clarity.
- Technology stack clarity.
- Real performance evidence if claimed.
- Accessibility and SEO readiness explained.

- Unsupported "high performance" claims.
- No clarity on WordPress/Shopify/Next.js boundaries.
- Weak project intake form.

### Primary Path

2. Clicks `/services`.
3. Reviews SEO, PPC/Google Ads and Social Media pages.

### Supporting Path

2. Reviews evaluation checklist.
3. Clicks Services.
4. Clicks Contact.

### Conversion Triggers

- "Book a strategy call."
- "Find the right service."
- "Talk to the team."

### Required Trust Signals

- Retainer process.
- Reporting cadence.
- Role clarity.
- Approved client testimonials.
- Evidence of multi-channel coordination.

### Friction To Remove

- Service cards that feel disconnected.
- No process for ongoing work.
- No proof of reporting/accountability.
- No clear next step.

## Form Journey Requirements

Recommended fields:

- Name.
- Email.
- Phone, optional unless owner confirms WhatsApp/phone workflow.
- Company.
- Website URL.
- Service interest.
- Budget range, optional and owner-approved.
- Message.
- Consent checkbox linked to privacy policy.

Do not display:

- Fake UAE phone number.
- Fake address.
- Fake consultation calendar.
- Fake client logos near the form.
