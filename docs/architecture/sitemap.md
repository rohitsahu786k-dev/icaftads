This sitemap is architecture only. Do not design or code pages yet.

## Conditional Pages

### `/digital-marketing-agency-dubai`

Status: Do not launch initially.

Reason:

- This page duplicates the homepage target intent: "digital marketing agency Dubai" and "digital marketing company Dubai."
- Launching both a homepage and a near-identical Dubai agency page would create cannibalization and thin-location-page risk.

Recommended use:

- Keep homepage as the broad commercial Dubai agency page.
- Consider `/digital-marketing-agency-dubai` only if the homepage must remain brand-led and this page can become a genuinely distinct long-form buyer guide with proof, service comparisons, FAQs and UAE business context.

### `/services/performance-marketing-dubai`

Status: Merge initially into `/services/ppc-google-ads-dubai`.

Reason:

- The requested PPC, Google Ads and performance marketing pages overlap heavily.
- Launching separate pages before proof exists risks thin content.

Recommended use:

- Use `/services/ppc-google-ads-dubai` as a paid media and performance service page.
- Split to `/services/performance-marketing-dubai` later only if I Craft Ads can provide distinct content around measurement strategy, CRO, creative testing, channel mix and approved case studies.

### `/services/branding`

Status: Conditional.

Reason:

- Existing site lists branding-related services, but branding requires portfolio proof and approved visual work.

Recommended use:

- Include in navigation only after owner supplies brand identity, naming, company profile or campaign examples.
- Until then, mention brand positioning as part of social/web strategy rather than a standalone page.

### `/services/marketing-automation`

Status: Merge initially.

Reason:

- Marketing automation and lead generation should be connected to CRM handoff, email, WhatsApp and funnel operations.

Recommended use:

- Launch combined `/services/lead-generation-marketing-automation-dubai`.
- Split later if owner supplies platform-specific expertise, workflows, screenshots and compliance documentation.
